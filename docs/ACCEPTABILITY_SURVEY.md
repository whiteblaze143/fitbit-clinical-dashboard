# ✅ Acceptability & Usability Survey (Pilot Feasibility)

Purpose: Collect participant feedback after the monitoring period to assess usability, acceptability, technology access, and equity considerations. Do not collect PHI. Use study IDs only.

---

## Administration
- Timing: After completion of monitoring (or early withdrawal)
- Format: Self-administered or coordinator-assisted
- Identifier: Study ID only (e.g., ABL01, CVR01)

---

## Core Items (Likert / Multiple Choice)

1. Study ID: __________
2. Estimated wear time (days): ____
3. Daily ECG adherence:
   - <25%
   - 25–50%
   - 50–75%
   - 75–90%
   - >90%
4. Ease of use of the smartwatch (1=very hard, 5=very easy): 1 2 3 4 5
5. Technical issues encountered (select all that apply):
   - Pairing
   - Battery life/charging
   - App navigation
   - ECG app use
   - Syncing
   - Other: __________
6. Comfort wearing the device (1=very uncomfortable, 5=very comfortable): 1 2 3 4 5
7. Likelihood to use a smartwatch for heart monitoring in the future (1=very unlikely, 5=very likely): 1 2 3 4 5

---

## Equity and Skin Tone (Optional)
- Please select the option that best reflects your skin tone:
  - Prefer not to say
  - I–II
  - III–IV
  - V–VI

Note: Skin tone is collected to explore potential differences in photoplethysmography (PPG) performance across skin tones. This is optional.

## Data Handling
- Store responses locally (e.g., CSV) on a Sunnybrook password-protected workstation.
- Use study IDs only; no names, emails, or other PHI.
- Aggregate results for reporting in the pilot feasibility analysis.

---

## In-Dashboard Capture (Optional)
The Streamlit dashboard includes a lightweight survey capture tool under “Acceptability & usability survey (optional)”. Use that module to collect and export CSV responses.
